# APQC Alignment

This is the governance point for aligning Autonomous SCM capabilities and processes with the APQC Process Classification Framework.

## Mapping Pattern

```text
APQC Process Area
    ↓
Process
    ↓
Capability
    ↓
Use Case
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Technology Enablement
```

Mappings should be maintained at the appropriate taxonomy level and should not force one-to-one relationships between process taxonomy and technology modules.
