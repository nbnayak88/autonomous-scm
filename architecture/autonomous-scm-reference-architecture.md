# Autonomous SCM Reference Architecture

## Purpose

Define the master reference architecture for Autonomous Supply Chain Management and establish the common architectural language shared by the 12 supply chain streams.

## Architecture Layers

1. Supply Chain Strategy and Business Outcomes
2. Value Streams and Capabilities
3. Process Architecture
4. People, Roles and Operating Model
5. Data and Information Architecture
6. Application Architecture
7. Technology Architecture
8. AI and Autonomous Decision Architecture
9. Integration and Event Architecture
10. Security, Risk and Governance
11. Analytics and Intelligence
12. Experience and Collaboration
13. Ecosystem and Platforms
14. Sustainability and Resilience

## Autonomous Decision Loop

```text
Sense → Understand → Predict → Decide → Act → Learn
  ↑                                           |
  └────────────── Feedback Loop ─────────────┘
```

The degree of autonomy should be determined per decision type, considering risk, data quality, controls, regulatory constraints, and human accountability.

## Governing Approach

Capability-led, process-centric, product-neutral, modular, data-centric, AI-enabled, API-oriented, event-aware, secure by design, sustainability-aware, and designed for continuous evolution.
