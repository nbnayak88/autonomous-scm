# Supply Chain Capability Model

The Autonomous SCM capability model organizes supply chain management around enduring business capabilities rather than software modules.

## Capability Domains

- Supply Chain Strategy & Design
- Demand Management & Planning
- Supply Planning & S&OP
- Manufacturing Operations
- Logistics & Transportation
- Inventory Management & Optimization
- Quality Management & Compliance
- Supplier Management & Collaboration
- Sustainability & ESG
- Supply Chain Analytics & Intelligence
- AI, Automation & Autonomous Supply Chain
- Supply Chain Integration & Enterprise Architecture

## Capability Design Pattern

```text
Business Outcome
      ↓
Capability
      ↓
Process
      ↓
Decision
      ↓
Data / Knowledge
      ↓
Application / Technology
      ↓
Automation / AI
      ↓
Measured Outcome
```
